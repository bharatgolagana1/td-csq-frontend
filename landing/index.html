The sample

A score you cannot write yourself.

You name your customers. You lock the sample before the window opens. After that you do not touch it. They are invited independently, they answer independently, and you never see who said what.

Three readings

The same terminal, read three ways.

The operator

Assess yourself against the full instrument as often as you like. Recorded, reported back to you, and deliberately excluded from the published score so it cannot flatter the number.

Your customers

The instrument

One instrument. Two directions.

International terminals0

Questions across infrastructure, security and safety, process, regulatory access and trade facilitation. Each answered twice, export and import, so a completed return carries 54 ratings.

Domestic terminals0

The same instrument adapted for domestic operations, rated inbound and outbound. Customs and bonded gate questions drop out. Everything else stays comparable.

Scale

Excellent to Poor, plus NA

Anything marked NA leaves the calculation rather than quietly dragging a category down.

Weighting

Set per cycle

Question weights and market share both feed the roll-up, so an airport with two operators of very different size still compares honestly.

Follow-up

Opens on a low rating

Rate a parameter Fair or Poor and the instrument asks what went wrong, so every weak score arrives with a reason attached.

The cycle

How a cycle runs.

Sampling

You build the list

Add or import your forwarders and customs brokers. No accounts exist yet. You then lock a sample no smaller than the cycle minimum.

Approval

The list is checked

The locked sample is approved automatically or reviewed first, depending on your account. Reviewers see every edit, who made it and when.

Opening night

Invitations go out

At midnight on the opening date every sampled assessor gets an email and a WhatsApp message. One tap, one code, straight into the form.

Window

Responses arrive

Assessors answer in one sitting or across several. Drafts save on their own. You watch the response rate climb without ever seeing an individual answer.

Close

The score publishes

Sampling closes, the auditor reading lands, the roll-up runs. You get the score, the gap, every comment, and your position against the field.

The standard

Built once. Read the same everywhere.

CSQ launches in India on the instrument it will carry everywhere else. A terminal in Bengaluru and a terminal in Amsterdam are read identically, because a score that is not comparable is not worth publishing.
